What you need

  • A device with a camera. A laptop, a computer with a webcam, a tablet or a phone. A bigger screen is easier to see from where you stand.
  • Chrome or Safari. The game opens in the browser, with no app to install.
  • About 2 metres of space. Step back until your whole upper body is in the picture, with light in front of you.

What happens to your camera picture

The game looks at the picture on your own device to find where your body is. The camera pictures never leave your device. You can read more on our privacy page.

Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. The games open in your browser, and there is no app to install.

Is it free?

Fruit Slicer is free to play, with no sign-in. One payment unlocks all 8 games and every new game later.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and Safari, on a computer, tablet or phone.

The game can't see me. What do I do?

Stand where your whole upper body is in the camera picture, with good light in front of you, not behind you.

The camera is off. How do I turn it on?

In Chrome, click the icon at the left end of the address bar and turn on "Camera". In Safari, or on a phone or tablet, open this website's settings from the address bar and allow the camera.
