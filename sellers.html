Earn money with Off the Couch

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: get your own link, share it, and earn 40% of each sale it brings, after the shop's fees. At $14.99 that is about $4.90 a sale.

How it works

Fill in the short form and tell us where you'll share the game. You need a free Gumroad account, which is how you get paid. Once we approve you, Gumroad sends you your own link.

When someone buys within 30 days of tapping your link, the sale is yours. Gumroad handles the payment and pays you directly once you've earned at least $100. If a buyer gets a refund, that sale's cut comes off your balance.

The rules

  • Say it's an ad, every time. Say and show "Ad: I earn money if you buy with my link" at the start of a video or post, turn on the platform's paid label, and write "Paid link" beside your link on a page.
  • Only say what's true. Fruit Slicer is free. The other seven games cost $14.99, paid once.
  • Talk to grown-ups. Never urge children to buy, or to ask their parents to buy.
  • No cut on your own purchase, or on purchases you make for other people.
  • No spam, coupon sites or browser add-ons, and no ads on the words "Off the Couch".
  • Don't sell or share game codes. Share your link only.

If you break these rules we can remove you. You're responsible for your own taxes and for following the rules where you live.
