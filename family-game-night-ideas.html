Family game night ideas that get everyone off the couch

Board games are great, until someone flips the board. These nine ideas get the whole family up and laughing, and most use what's already in the house. One is a game we made, and we've marked it.

9 family game night ideas

  • Charades. Write movies or animals on paper, split into teams and race the clock.
  • Paper plate balloon tennis. Paper plates are the rackets, a balloon is the ball, the couch is the net.
  • Minute challenges. Stack ten cups, or move cotton balls with a spoon, before the minute is up.
  • Draw it big. Tape paper to the wall and play a drawing game standing up, with a timer.
  • Flashlight hide and seek. Lights off. Whoever is seeking gets the flashlight.
  • Sock toss. Everyone gets five rolled socks. Laundry baskets at different distances are worth different points.
  • Paper plane contest. Everyone folds one. Longest flight wins, then best trick.
  • Living room Olympics. Pick four silly events and hand out paper medals at the end.
  • A score battle on the big screen (made by us). Off the Couch uses your camera instead of a controller, so everyone can play without anyone buying anything to hold. Take turns and keep score.

How to run a score battle

  • Set it up: open the game on a computer, tablet or phone with a camera. A laptop plugged into the TV by cable makes the picture big.
  • Clear the floor: push the coffee table back. The player stands about 2 meters (6 feet) from the camera.
  • Take turns: the game reads one player at a time, so go around the room and write each score down.
  • Add a prize: the lowest score makes the popcorn.
