Off the Couch

Arcade for your body

Games you play with your whole body, using your Mac's camera. No controller needed.

Help

The game can't see me. Stand where your whole upper body is in the camera picture, with good light in front of you, not behind you.

The camera is off. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, and turn on Off the Couch.

I bought "Unlock all games" before. Open Off the Couch, choose "Settings" on the menu, then "Restore purchase".
