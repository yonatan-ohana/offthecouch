Help

Questions?

Something else? Write to support@cuzgames.com and tell us what you see.

The game can't see me.

Stand where your whole upper body is in the camera picture, with good light in front of you, not behind you.

What do I need?

A phone or tablet, or a computer with a camera and Google Chrome, to play in the browser. Then about 2 metres of space.

The camera is off.

In Chrome: click the icon at the left end of the address bar and turn on "Camera".

I bought the games before.

In the browser: choose "Already bought? Sign in" under the price on this page, or "Already bought? Log in" on the game's menu, and sign in the way you did when you bought. In the Mac app: choose "Settings" on the menu, then "Restore purchase".
