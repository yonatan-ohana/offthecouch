Accessibility

Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short: we want this website to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or bigger text. The games themselves need you to see the screen and move your arms. If something doesn't work for you, tell us and we'll fix it.

This website

The pages are plain text with real links and buttons, so they work with a keyboard and with screen readers. Pictures that carry meaning have a written description, the text can be made bigger in your browser, and the moving videos stop if your device is set to show less motion. We aim to meet the Israeli accessibility standard (IS 5568, based on the international WCAG 2.0 AA guidelines). No outside expert has checked the website yet, so some things may still be missed.

The games

Off the Couch is played by moving your body in front of the camera: you need to see the screen and move at least your arms. That means the games can't be played with a screen reader or without moving. The menus can also be used with a mouse or by touch. If you have an idea that would let more people play, we'd love to hear it.

Tell us about a problem

Write to support@cuzgames.com and tell us which page, what you were trying to do and what went wrong. We'll answer as soon as we can.
