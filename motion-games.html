Motion games used to mean a console, a special camera and a cable behind the TV. Off the Couch needs only the device you already have. Jump, duck, lean and wave, and the game follows you.

Looking for games like Kinect or EyeToy?

The idea is the same: your body is the controller. The difference is that there is nothing to buy or plug in. The camera in your laptop or tablet does the job, and the game runs in your browser.

Off the Couch is not connected to Kinect, EyeToy or their makers.

Questions

Do I need a console or a VR headset?

No. You need a computer, tablet or phone with a camera, and Chrome or Safari.

Can two people play at once?

The games are played one at a time, so take turns.

How much space do I need?

About 2 metres around you. Mind furniture, pets and little ones.

Is it free?

Fruit Slicer is free to play, with no sign-in. One payment unlocks all 8 games.

Do the games work on a phone?

Yes, in Chrome or Safari, but a bigger screen is easier to see from 2 metres away.
