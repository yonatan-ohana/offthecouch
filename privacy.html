Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short: the camera only sees how you move, and the pictures never leave your device. We collect nothing about you unless you buy in the browser, and then only what's needed to open your games on your devices.

The camera

Off the Couch uses your camera so your body can control the games, in the Mac app and in the browser, on a computer, phone or tablet. Each picture is looked at on your own device, straight away, to find where your arms, head and body are, and then thrown away. Pictures are never saved, never recorded and never sent anywhere: not to us, and not to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The games keep a few small things so they remember them next time: your stars, the stickers you have, your best scores, and your sound and music settings. The Mac app keeps them on your Mac; in the browser they're kept in your browser's storage for this website. If you sign in in the browser, it also keeps a random name for this device and a sign-in key. None of this is sent to us, except the sign-in key and the device's random name when you play the paid games. Deleting the app, or clearing this website's data in your browser, deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you buy in the browser. There are no ads and no tracking, and this website sets no cookies of its own. Only when you choose to buy or sign in, the services that do that (Google sign-in, the Paddle payment window and Cloudflare's robot check, below) open and may set their own cookies, which they need to work. To know how many people visit and which pages or links brought them, we count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, doesn't follow you to other websites and doesn't build a profile of you; we only see totals. On the play page we also count how far visits get (the page opened, the camera allowed, a person seen, a round started or finished, the unlock window opened) as daily totals on our own server, with nothing about who: no internet address, no account, no camera picture. To play for free you never sign in.

Buying in the browser

To open the games on any device you use, you sign in with Google or with a phone number when you buy. Then we keep:

  • With Google: your Google account's email address and its account number. Google shares only these with us, and only when you choose "Continue with Google". We never see your Google password.
  • With a phone number: a scrambled copy of the number (it can't be turned back into the number) and its last four digits, so you can recognise it in "Your account". The number itself is kept only until you type the code we text you. The code is texted by Twilio, which receives the number for that, under its own privacy notice.
  • Your purchase: whether you bought, when, and the order number. The order itself is handled by Paddle.com, our online reseller: Paddle collects your payment details and email address under its own privacy notice, and shares with us your email address, country and order details. We use them only to open your games, answer your emails and handle refunds. We never see your card details.
  • Your signed-in devices: a scrambled copy of each device's sign-in key, its random name, and when it was last used. The games play on one device at a time, so we also note which of your devices is playing right now.
  • Internet addresses: to stop people sending lots of texts or sign-ins, we count them per internet address for one day, then delete the count.

Before we text a code to sign in on another device, a short check by Cloudflare Turnstile makes sure a person, not a program, is asking. Cloudflare also runs the program that keeps all of the above, under its own privacy policy.

We never sell or share this with anyone else, and never use it for ads.

Deleting your account

In the game, choose "Settings", then "Your account", then "Delete my account". This deletes your sign-in, your email address or phone number, and your signed-in devices straight away, and the paid games lock on every device. We keep only the order number, because the law requires sales records; Paddle keeps its own order records for the same reason. You can also write to us and we'll delete it for you.

"Sign out of this computer" in the same place signs out only that device.

Buying in the Mac app

"Unlock all games" in the Mac app is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, and there is nothing to sign in to.

The website and the game engine

This website is hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard records of visits (such as internet addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The games are made with Unity, which may receive basic, anonymous technical details about your device (such as its model and system version) when a game starts. This is never linked to you and never includes camera pictures.

Children

Children can play for free without giving us anything. Buying and signing in in the browser should be done by an adult.

Your rights

You can ask us what we keep about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Write to privacy@cuzgames.com.
