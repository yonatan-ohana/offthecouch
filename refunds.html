Refunds

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: bought in the browser? Ask within 14 days and you get your money back. Bought in an app? That app's store handles refunds.

Bought in the browser

If you're not happy, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying and you'll get your money back, no questions asked. Write to support@cuzgames.com with the email address you used and the order number from your receipt, or use the link in the receipt email from Paddle. Paddle, our online reseller, makes the refund, and the money goes back the way you paid. After a refund, the games lock again in the browser on every device. Deleting your account doesn't refund you, so ask for the refund first.

Bought in the Mac app

Purchases in the Mac App Store are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. Apple decides on these refunds; we can't refund App Store purchases ourselves.

Try before you buy

Fruit Slicer is free everywhere, so you can check that the games work with your camera and your room before you pay.
