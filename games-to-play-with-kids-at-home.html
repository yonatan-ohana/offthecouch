With nothing at all

  • Simon says. Let your child be Simon. They will enjoy being in charge.
  • Red light, green light. Walk on green and freeze on red, down the hallway.
  • Statues. Spin, stop and hold a pose. Anyone who wobbles is out.
  • I spy. Good when you need to sit down for a minute.
  • Hide and seek. Still the best game ever invented.

With a pen and paper

  • Dots and boxes. Take turns joining dots. Whoever closes a box claims it.
  • Drawing guesses. One draws, the other guesses, and you swap every minute.
  • Story chain. Each person adds one sentence, then folds the paper so the next person sees only the last line.

With things around the house

  • Sock basketball. Rolled socks and a laundry basket.
  • Cup stacking. Build the tallest tower, then race to take it down.
  • Spoon race. Carry a ball of paper on a spoon across the room.
