10 rainy day activities that get kids moving

  • Sock basketball. Roll up socks and use a laundry basket as the hoop. Take a step back after every basket.
  • Hallway bowling. Line up empty plastic bottles and roll a soft ball at them.
  • The floor is lava. Lay out cushions as stepping stones and cross the room without touching the floor.
  • Tape balance line. Stick masking tape along the floor. Walk it forward, then backward, then with a book on your head.
  • Balloon keep-up. Don't let the balloon touch the floor. To make it harder, allow only elbows or knees.
  • Freeze dance. Play music, stop it without warning, and anyone still moving sits out a turn.
  • Scavenger hunt. Write a list: something red, something soft, something older than you. First back with everything wins.
  • Living room obstacle course. Under the table, over the cushions, around the chair, then back again against the clock.
  • Animal walk races. Cross the room as a crab, then a bear, then a frog.
  • A game you play with your whole body (made by us). Off the Couch turns the camera on your computer, tablet or phone into the controller. Stand back and slice falling fruit with your hands. There's nothing to hold and nothing to install.

Which one, right now?

  • Five minutes and no setup: freeze dance or animal walks.
  • One child on their own: tape balance line, or the fruit game.
  • A small apartment: balloon keep-up or the tape line. Both fit in a hallway.
  • A few kids at once: the obstacle course or the scavenger hunt.
